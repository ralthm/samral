# Samral

Build a modern, clean landing page for samral.com.

Samral is the parent brand / holdco that houses multiple startups and ventures. The website should feel simple, sharp, modern, and founder-led — not corporate, not overly flashy, and not too agency-like. Think minimalist venture studio / personal holding company.

The main goal of the site is to introduce Samral and showcase the startups currently under it.

Current startups to feature:

SubsNest
Website: www.subsnest.com
Description: A subscription tracking app that helps users track subscriptions, avoid forgotten charges, and stay in control of recurring spending.

TripHalfsies
Website: www.triphalfsies.com
Description: A travel expense splitting app that makes it easy for groups to track shared trip expenses and settle up fairly.

PAGE STRUCTURE

1. HERO SECTION

Headline:
Building useful digital products, one venture at a time.

Subheadline:
Samral is the home for the startups and tools I’m building — focused on solving simple, real-world problems through software.

Primary CTA button:
Explore Ventures

Secondary CTA button:
Get in Touch

Keep the hero clean and centered, with a premium but minimalist feel.

2. ABOUT SECTION

Title:
What is Samral?

Text:
Samral is a holding company for the digital products and ventures I’m building. The focus is on creating practical, easy-to-use tools that solve everyday problems and can grow into lasting businesses.

Optional second paragraph:
Right now, Samral includes early-stage software products in personal finance and travel, with more ventures to come over time.

3. VENTURES SECTION

Title:
Current Ventures

Create 2 clean, modern cards for the two startups.

Card 1: SubsNest

Title:
SubsNest

Description:
A subscription tracking app that helps people manage recurring payments, avoid forgotten charges, and stay on top of monthly spending.

Button:
Visit SubsNest

Link button to:
https://www.subsnest.com

Optional small tag:
Personal Finance

Card 2: TripHalfsies

Title:
TripHalfsies

Description:
A travel expense splitting app that helps friends and groups track shared costs, split expenses fairly, and settle up easily during trips.

Button:
Visit TripHalfsies

Link button to:
https://www.triphalfsies.com

Optional small tag:
Travel / Expense Sharing

4. VISION SECTION

Title:
The Long-Term Vision

Text:
Samral is being built as a platform to launch and grow multiple ventures over time. The focus is on starting small, validating quickly, and building products that people genuinely find useful.

Optional second paragraph:
As new ideas are developed, they will be added under the Samral umbrella.

5. CONTACT / FOOTER CTA SECTION

Title:
Interested in what I’m building?

Text:
Whether you want to follow the journey, connect, or explore future collaborations, feel free to reach out.

Button:
Contact

If no contact form is added yet, make this a simple placeholder button or mailto link.

DESIGN INSTRUCTIONS

Clean, modern, premium, minimalist

Mobile-first and responsive

Founder-led feel, not corporate

Soft shadows, rounded cards, plenty of whitespace

Neutral color palette with a classy modern startup aesthetic

Easy to scan quickly

Avoid clutter and unnecessary animations

Make the ventures section the visual centerpiece

Use subtle hover effects on venture cards and buttons

Keep typography sharp and professional

OPTIONAL ENHANCEMENTS

If suitable, add:

A subtle line near the hero such as:
Current focus: software products in finance, travel, and everyday utility

A small “More ventures coming soon” note below the venture cards

Simple icons for each venture category

A very light premium background texture or gradient, but keep it understated

TONE

The tone should feel:

ambitious

modern

thoughtful

founder-led

simple and credible

Do not make it sound overly corporate, vague, or filled with buzzwords.
