# Samral homepage editorial refinement

## Proposed structure

1. **Hero**
   - Keep the existing logo, full-bleed hero image, headline, supporting sentence, two actions, price, and delivery promise.
   - Remove the empty expandable copy and avoid adding badges or extra claims.

2. **How it works**
   - Keep the three numbered steps, but simplify them into a flat editorial sequence separated by thin rules.
   - Use the requested wording and remove the extra section headline that repeats the promise.

3. **Client results**
   - Move this directly after the process and make the two verified outcomes the visual focus.
   - Preserve the Synapse and Charoen Pokphand Foods logos and existing factual outcomes.
   - Use Samuel's first-person voice for work he performed, then keep the linked personal-review statement directly below the cases.

4. **Services**
   - Keep Points Trip Planning at US$99 and Personal Card Strategy at US$149.
   - Remove the unnecessary tailoring sentence and tighten each service to the supplied description, four deliverables, scope, timing, and action.
   - Present them as two editorial service columns rather than subscription-style pricing cards.

5. **Free Miles Calculator**
   - Add a substantial homepage section using real calculator data and a small authentic interactive preview powered by existing calculation logic.
   - Show only statistics computed from the current live data exports; do not hard-code marketing counts or duplicate calculator rules.
   - Link to the full calculator without changing its behavior.

6. **For business owners**
   - Reduce the homepage section to the supplied short proposition and one link.
   - Remove the five-stage methodology from the homepage only; keep the full Business page unchanged.

7. **About Samuel**
   - Replace the travel collage and philosophy copy with the existing real Samuel portrait, concise supplied copy, and About link.

8. **Remove “The idea”**
   - Delete the standalone Kyoto/philosophy section without replacement.

9. **Final action**
   - Keep the existing atmospheric closing treatment, but shorten it to the supplied heading, two service actions, and calculator link.

## Visual approach

- Preserve the current warm paper, ink, clay, serif display type, restrained photography, and square/low-radius controls.
- Build hierarchy with typography, whitespace, alignment, thin dividers, real logos, real outcomes, and real calculator information.
- Reduce cards, labels, shadows, decorative imagery, and repeated centered layouts.
- Keep headings controlled on mobile, prices visible, tap targets accessible, results scannable, and the calculator preview free of horizontal overflow.

## Existing functionality and assets affected

- **Preserved unchanged:** Tally URL and tracking, Cal.com route and payment flow, Miles Calculator page and calculations, navigation/footer behavior, analytics events, internal links, SEO unless copy consistency requires a description update.
- **Reused:** current hero image, Samuel portrait, Synapse logo, Charoen Pokphand Foods logo, commerce prices and delivery constants, calculator data and calculation functions.
- **Removed from homepage only:** travel-photo collage, Kyoto “The idea” section, duplicate philosophy copy, and Business methodology list.
- No new service, statistic, testimonial, client outcome, or image will be invented.
